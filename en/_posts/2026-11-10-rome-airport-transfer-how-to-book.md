After a long trip, the one thing you want in a big city is to know someone is waiting for you. A pre-booked transfer is exactly that.

## What to tell us when you book
- **Airport and terminal** of arrival (Fiumicino or Ciampino).
- **Flight number**, so the driver can follow any delay without you having to call.
- **Number of passengers and bags**, including oversized ones: it decides which car fits.
- The full **destination address**, ideally with the name of the hotel or property.
- A **phone number** you can be reached on after landing.

## What happens on arrival
Your driver waits at the agreed meeting point, usually holding a sign with your name. They help with your luggage and walk you to the car. If your flight is late, the service adjusts, which is why the flight number comes first.

## The price
With a chauffeur service the price is agreed **before the ride**. Always ask in advance and check what it includes: waiting time, luggage, any stops.

## Mistakes to avoid
1. Booking at the last minute without a flight number.
2. Not mentioning that child seats are needed.
3. Forgetting a working phone number.
