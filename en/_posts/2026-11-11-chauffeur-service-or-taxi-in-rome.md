There are two common ways to get around Rome: a taxi and a chauffeur service (NCC in Italian). They look alike, but they work differently.

## How you get the service
- **Taxi**: picked up at a rank or called on the spot.
- **Chauffeur service**: booked in advance with route, date and time. The driver arrives where and when you agreed.

## The price
- **Taxi**: follows the fares set for the service.
- **Chauffeur service**: the amount is agreed before departure, so you know what you will pay before you get in.

## When a chauffeur service makes sense
- You are arriving by plane and want someone waiting for you.
- You travel as a group or with a lot of luggage.
- You have a fixed time to meet (meeting, train, ceremony).
- You want a route with several stops, planned with the driver.

## When a taxi is enough
For a short, unplanned ride with no schedule to keep, a taxi is still the quickest option.

In short: a taxi is for the ride of the moment, a chauffeur service is for the one you want to get right.
