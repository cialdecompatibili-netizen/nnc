A day in Rome goes fast, especially with queues, distances and heat. With a driver at your disposal you lose less time and choose where to stop.

## Before you set off
Pick two or three main stops with your driver, no more. Rome is meant to be walked: better to see a little well than rush from place to place.

## How to shape the day
1. **Morning**: the busiest area, while crowds are still thin.
2. **Lunch**: a quiet spot, even outside the centre.
3. **Afternoon**: a more relaxed stop, on foot or by car.
4. **Evening**: back to the hotel or out to dinner, without worrying about parking.

## Beyond Rome
With a car at your disposal you can add the surroundings: nearby villages, castles and hills are within reach on a tailor-made route.

## Practical tips
- Bring water and comfortable shoes.
- Check opening hours for the places you want to visit.
- Tell us early if anyone has reduced mobility.
