On your wedding day, getting around should not become a worry. A bit of early planning is enough.

## The couple's car
Agree on departure time, arrival point and some spare time before the ceremony. The driver knows the route and takes care of everything, including stops for photos.

## Your guests
- If the ceremony and the reception are in different places, a shuttle spares everyone the parking problem.
- For the ride home in the evening, a pre-booked service lets everyone enjoy the party without driving.

## What to include when you ask for a quote
1. Date and times of the ceremony and the reception.
2. Pick-up, drop-off and any stop addresses.
3. Number of guests to transport.
4. Special requests: decorations, photo stops, return times.

## One tip
Write to us well in advance: for the most popular dates it is better to book early.
