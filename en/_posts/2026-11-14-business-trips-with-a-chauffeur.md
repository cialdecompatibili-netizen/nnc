For people travelling on business, time matters more than distance. A dedicated driver lets you arrive rested and use the ride to prepare.

## What to check
- **Punctuality**: a clear pick-up time and a direct contact for any surprise.
- **Flexibility**: the option to move the time if your meeting runs long.
- **Discretion**: work gets discussed in the car, so the driver must be discreet.
- **Paperwork**: if you need a receipt or an invoice, ask when you book.

## For frequent travellers
An arrangement on recurring routes (hotel, office, airport) makes everything simpler: same contacts, same way to request a ride.

## Welcoming clients and guests
A car with a driver is also a good way to host people visiting you, from arrival through to dinner.
