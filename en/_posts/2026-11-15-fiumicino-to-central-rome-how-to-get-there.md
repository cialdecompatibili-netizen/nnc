Right after landing at Fiumicino the question is always the same: how do I get into the city? There are three main ways, each with pros and cons.

## The train
There is a rail link that takes you into town, up to Termini station. It is a good choice if you travel light, already know where you are going and your destination is near a station.

**Keep in mind:** after the train you will almost always need another ride to reach your hotel, especially with luggage.

## The taxi
You find it at the exit with no booking. It is handy if you have no fixed plans.

**Keep in mind:** in high season the queue can be long, and you do not know in advance who you will get or how payment works.

## A car with a driver
Booked in advance: on arrival someone is waiting with your name, helps with your bags and takes you right to your door. The price is set beforehand.

**Keep in mind:** it must be requested before you travel, with your flight number.

## Which to choose
1. **Travelling alone, light and in no hurry:** the train may be enough.
2. **Arriving tired, with family or lots of luggage:** a car with a driver is better.
3. **Landing late at night or at dawn:** booking ahead removes every doubt.
