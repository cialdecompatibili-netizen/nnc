Civitavecchia is the port many Rome cruises sail from and arrive at. On boarding or disembarkation day every minute counts, and a transfer that is already arranged takes one worry off your list.

## Before boarding
- Check the **boarding time** given by the cruise line and leave a margin.
- If you arrive by plane, share your **flight number**: the driver follows any delay.
- If you have lots of luggage, say so when you book.

## On disembarkation day
After disembarking you usually have a flight or train to catch. Agree the pick-up time while allowing for the time it takes to leave the ship, which can vary.

## A day in Rome before or after your cruise
If you have a few hours, you can build in a stop in Rome: a short tour, lunch, then the transfer to the airport or the port, all in the same car with no changes.

## What to include for a quote
1. Date and time of boarding or disembarkation.
2. Starting point and destination (airport, station, hotel, port).
3. Number of passengers and bags.
4. Any intermediate stops.
