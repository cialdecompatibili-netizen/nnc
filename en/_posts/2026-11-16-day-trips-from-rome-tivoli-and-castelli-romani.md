Rome alone could fill a week, but if you have an extra day you can leave the city and see a different side of the region. Two classic destinations, close by and perfect for a day.

## Tivoli
Very near Rome, it is famous for its villas and gardens: among the best known are Villa d'Este and Hadrian's Villa. A good trip for lovers of history, gardens and fountains.

## The Castelli Romani
A hill area south-east of Rome, with towns such as Frascati, Castel Gandolfo and Albano Laziale. Choose it for cooler air, views over the lakes and good food.

## Why go with a driver
- No parking to find in narrow historic centres.
- You can add or skip a stop, free from public transport timetables.
- Lunch and wine tasting without worrying about driving.

## How to plan the day
1. Pick one main destination.
2. Leave some margin between visits.
3. Check opening hours for the places you want to see.
