A great evening can be spoilt by thinking about parking or about who has to stay sober. With a driver, the evening starts the moment you leave home.

## When it makes sense
- **Dinners** in central venues, where parking is hard.
- **Events and shows**, with pick-up and drop-off at the same spot.
- **Birthdays and celebrations**, when you want to toast without worries.
- **Group nights out**, to arrive and leave all together.

## How to organise it
1. State the **pick-up time and place** and the destination.
2. Agree the **return time**, with some margin: if the evening runs long, tell us and we adapt.
3. Give the **number of people**, so we choose the right car.

## The ride home
The biggest advantage is the return: you get in the car and arrive home, without deciding who drives and without waiting for a taxi late at night.

## For groups
With several people, a single vehicle keeps everyone together and nobody gets left behind.
