With children, every trip works better when it is planned. A family-friendly transfer spares you the most tiring parts of the journey.

## What to tell us when you book
- **Age and number of children**, so the car is prepared properly.
- **Car seat or booster**: ask in advance, or say if you bring your own.
- **Stroller and luggage**: needed to choose a car with enough room.

## For a calmer journey
1. Carry water, a snack and a toy or a book.
2. If the ride is long, agree a short stop.
3. Keep documents and the flight number within reach.

## On arrival
The driver helps with suitcases and the stroller, so you can focus on the children. No taxi queue after a long flight.

## Sightseeing as a family
If you want to see Rome with children, a car at your disposal lets you make short stops, go back to the hotel for a break and set off again without rush.

Tell us how many you are and the children's ages: we prepare everything before you arrive.
